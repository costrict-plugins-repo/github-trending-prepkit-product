# Opportunity Shaping Patterns

## Good Opportunity Statement

"Users trying to [job] struggle because [friction], which leads to [cost or risk]."

Examples:
- "Users trying to compare total checkout cost struggle because shipping fees appear too late, which causes abandonment."
- "Managers trying to follow project health struggle because status updates arrive in inconsistent formats, which creates blind spots."

## Bad Opportunity Statement

- "Build a shipping fee preview component"
- "Add a better dashboard"

Rule:
- If the statement names the solution, rewrite it until it names the user struggle instead.
