# Pursue, Monitor, Defer

## Decision Meanings

| State | Meaning | Typical Next Move |
|---|---|---|
| Pursue | Evidence and timing are strong enough to shape execution | `PRD` or detailed prioritization |
| Monitor | Interesting, but more signal or dependency progress is needed | keep visible with a trigger |
| Defer | Not now, or not worth current investment | record why and when to revisit |

## Decision Rules

- `Pursue` when the problem is evidenced, the opportunity is meaningful, and dependencies are acceptable
- `Monitor` when the signal is promising but not strong enough for commitment
- `Defer` when timing, dependency, or value case is weak relative to alternatives

Always record:
- why the status was chosen
- what would change the status
