# JTBD Interview Framework

## Interview Timeline — Question Trees

### 1. The First Thought
Anchor to the moment the user knew something needed to change.
- "When did you first realise you needed something different?"
- "What was happening in your work/life at that point?"
- "Had anything changed recently that made this feel urgent?"

### 2. Passive Looking
Surface what they considered before actively searching.
- "Before you started looking, what solutions crossed your mind?"
- "Did you try any workarounds? What were they?"
- "How long did you live with the problem before looking for a fix?"

### 3. Active Looking
Identify the triggering event that moved them from passive to active.
- "What was the moment you decided to act?"
- "What happened that day / that week?"
- "How did you search? What did you look for?"

### 4. Deciding
Surface what almost stopped the switch.
- "What almost stopped you from switching?"
- "What did you have to give up to make this change?"
- "Who else was involved in the decision?"

## Switching Forces Diagram

```
PUSH (why they leave today)          PULL (why they choose new)
  Current pain, frequency, severity    New attraction, promise, social proof

ANXIETY (fear of switching)          HABIT (comfort of staying)
  Learning cost, risk of failure        "Good enough", sunk cost, familiarity
```

All four forces must be explored. A strong PULL with weak PUSH = no switch.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "What features do you want?" | Puts user in solution space | Ask about past behaviour, not future desires |
| Accepting "nice to have" | No urgency = no real job | Ask "what did you do instead? how painful was that?" |
| Skipping emotional context | Jobs have functional AND emotional dimensions | Ask "how did that make you feel?" after every pain |
| One interview, one persona | Switching forces vary by segment | Conduct 5+ interviews per distinct user type |
| Summarising before quoting | Loses signal in paraphrase | Capture verbatim quotes, tag by force type |
