# User Research Patterns

## Method Selection — Decision Table

| Situation | Method | Min. Sample | Typical Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand motivations, context, switching forces | User Interview | 5–8 per segment | 1–2 weeks |
| Validate patterns across broader population | Survey | 100+ | 1–2 weeks |
| Find usability problems in a flow | Usability Test | 5 | 1 week |
| Understand actual usage behaviour | Analytics Review | N/A (event data) | Ongoing |
| Prioritise features by satisfaction vs. expectation | Kano Survey | 30+ | 1 week |
| Test a specific hypothesis quickly | Fake Door / Smoke Test | 200+ impressions | 3–5 days |

Rule of thumb: qualitative first (interviews) → quantitative to confirm (survey/analytics).

---

## Question Framing Rules

| Good | Bad | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Walk me through the last time you did X" | "Do you often struggle with X?" | Behavioural recall vs. leading |
| "What did you do when that happened?" | "Would you use a feature that did Y?" | Past behaviour predicts; hypothetical doesn't |
| "How did that make you feel?" | "That must have been frustrating, right?" | Open probe vs. confirmation |
| "Tell me more about that" | "So you mean you wanted Z?" | Expand vs. interpret prematurely |

## Synthesis Patterns

**Affinity Mapping**
1. One observation per sticky (digital or physical)
2. Group by theme without pre-labelling groups
3. Name groups after clustering, using user language not internal jargon
4. Count recurrence — frequency signals strength of the need

**Theme Extraction Checklist**
- [ ] Themes named in user language (not product terminology)
- [ ] Each theme has at least 3 supporting quotes
- [ ] Themes distinguish between segments (note which persona said what)
- [ ] Emotional signal captured alongside functional signal

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation bias | Only hear what confirms existing plan | Assign devil's advocate in synthesis sessions |
| Leading questions | Data reflects researcher's view, not user's | Pre-review interview guide with someone outside the team |
| Small sample overgeneralisation | 2 interviews treated as universal truth | State n= explicitly; label findings as signal not proof until validated |
| Skipping synthesis | Raw notes never become actionable insight | Dedicate synthesis session within 24h of final interview |
| Surveying before interviewing | Survey measures the wrong things | Always interview first to learn what to measure |
