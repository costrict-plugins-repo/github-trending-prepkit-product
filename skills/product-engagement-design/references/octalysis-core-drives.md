# Octalysis Core Drives

Yu-kai Chou's motivation model mapped to product mechanics. Use to choose a small number of reinforcing drives, not to stack every lever at once.

## Drive Reference Table

| Drive | White Hat Use | Black Hat Risk | Primary Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epic Meaning | mission, contribution, impact | fake urgency | community and social-impact products |
| Accomplishment | mastery, levels, badges | hollow achievements | learning and productivity |
| Empowerment | sandbox, creativity, feedback | disguised restriction | creative and builder tools |
| Ownership | customization, collections | sunk-cost exploitation | marketplace and creator products |
| Social Influence | collaboration, mentorship | shame or toxic ranking | communities and team products |
| Scarcity | earned exclusives, seasons | fake countdowns, forced FOMO | limited programs and launches |
| Unpredictability | surprise, exploration | opaque reward odds | content and exploration loops |
| Loss Avoidance | streak protection, comeback boosts | punishment and guilt | habits and health behaviors |

## Design Priority

Prefer drives 2-5 for sustainable motivation. Use 1, 6, 7, and 8 sparingly and only with explicit guardrails.

## SDT Alignment Check

Before shipping a drive-based mechanic, verify it supports at least one:

| Pillar | Question |
|---|---|
| Autonomy | Does the user still have meaningful choice? |
| Competence | Does the user gain real skill or progress? |
| Relatedness | Does this create healthy connection? |

If none apply, the design is likely manipulative.

## Combination Recipes

| Product Type | Primary Drives | Supporting Drives |
|---|---|---|
| Learning | Accomplishment + Empowerment | Loss Avoidance with forgiveness |
| Community | Social Influence + Ownership | Epic Meaning |
| Creative tool | Empowerment + Ownership | Accomplishment |
| Habit app | Loss Avoidance + Accomplishment | Social Influence |

## Common Mistakes

- activating too many drives at once
- relying only on Black Hat drives
- skipping ethical review on scarcity, unpredictability, or loss mechanics
- choosing drives by designer taste instead of user motivation
