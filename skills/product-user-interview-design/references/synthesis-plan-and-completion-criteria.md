# Synthesis Plan And Completion Criteria

## Synthesis Plan

- Tag each interview by problem signal, current alternative, and confidence impact
- Capture verbatim quotes before summarizing patterns
- Note what changed in the team's decision after each interview batch

## Completion Criteria

Stop when one of these is true:
- the blocked decision is no longer meaningfully ambiguous
- the same problem pattern repeats across enough participants for the chosen segment
- the research clearly points to `defer` or `reject`

## Minimum Safeguards

- Use at least 5 interviews per distinct segment unless a strong negative signal emerges earlier
- Review after every 2-3 interviews to see whether the decision is already moving
- If the evidence gap remains unchanged after the planned interviews, escalate back to facilitation for a different method
