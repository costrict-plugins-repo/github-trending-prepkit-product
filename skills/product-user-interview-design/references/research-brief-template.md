# Research Brief Template

Use this structure for `research/research-brief.md`.

## Decision To Validate
- What decision is blocked?
- What would change if the answer is "yes"?
- What would change if the answer is "no"?

## Current Evidence
- What do we already know?
- What is still assumed?
- Why are interviews the right next step?

## Participant Target
- Who should be recruited?
- What context or behavior must they have experienced?
- Who should be excluded?

## Expected Outcome
- What signal would move us to `PRD`, `experiment`, `defer`, or `reject`?
