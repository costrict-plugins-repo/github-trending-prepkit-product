# AARRR Pirate Metrics

## Funnel Stages

| Stage | Question | Primary Metric | Counter-Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | How do users find us? | Signup rate by channel | Cost per acquisition |
| Activation | Do they have a good first experience? | Onboarding completion rate | Time-to-activate |
| Retention | Do they come back? | D30 retention rate | Support ticket volume |
| Revenue | Do they pay? | Paid conversion rate | Churn rate |
| Referral | Do they tell others? | Invite / share rate | Spam complaints |

---

## Stage Diagnostics

Use this to find your current bottleneck before optimising anything:

1. Acquisition high, Activation low → onboarding or value discovery problem
2. Activation high, Retention low → habit formation or frequency problem
3. Retention high, Revenue low → monetisation or pricing model problem
4. Revenue high, Referral low → advocacy or NPS problem; growth ceiling approaching

Fix the earliest broken stage first. Optimising Referral with a broken Retention floor wastes effort.

---

## Key Metrics Per Stage

**Activation**
- Aha-moment rate: % of signups who reach the core value moment within session 1
- D1 retention: users who return the day after signup

**Retention**
- D1 / D7 / D30 retention curves
- Churn rate (monthly/annual)
- Resurrection rate: churned users who return

**Revenue**
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)
- LTV / CAC ratio (healthy = >3:1)
- Expansion revenue rate

---

## When AARRR Does Not Apply

| Context | Problem | Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-product-market-fit | Funnel data is noise | Focus on Activation + D7 Retention only |
| Enterprise / long sales cycle | Funnel model breaks across months | Use deal-stage pipeline metrics instead |
| Two-sided marketplace | Single funnel misses supply-side health | Run separate funnels for each side |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Optimising Acquisition before Retention is healthy | Filling a leaky bucket — growth spend wasted |
| Measuring all stages equally | No focus; spreads team across every problem |
| No counter-metrics per stage | Metric manipulation goes undetected |
| Treating D1 retention as sufficient proxy for habit | D1 is signal; D30 is habit — track both |
